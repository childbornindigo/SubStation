# LuxuryLane Tires -- FULL Pricing Audit (All Sizes, All Models)

**Date:** May 17, 2026
**Scope:** All 21 tire models, ALL sizes checked against Canadian online pricing
**Competitors checked:** canadiantire.ca, 1010tires.com, pmctire.com, quattrotires.com, blackcircles.ca, 4tires.ca, wheelsco.ca, tdotperformance.ca, point-s.ca, v1auto.ca
**Methodology:** WebSearch + WebFetch across 10 Canadian tire retailers; prices in CAD per tire.
**Previous audit:** Checked only 29 featured sizes. THIS audit covers representative sizes across ALL rim diameters for each model.

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**1,911 tires audited across 21 models. Systemic overpricing found in 12 of 21 models.**

The previous audit only checked one "featured" size per model. This full audit reveals that the overpricing problem extends far beyond the featured sizes -- many models have EVERY size overpriced because TDG wholesale costs are structurally uncompetitive vs Canadian online retail.

### Critical Findings

1. **Continental ExtremeContact DWS06 PLUS (73 sizes):** Overpriced by $10-$150 per tire across EVERY size. 4tires.ca and Wheels Collection undercut us on every single SKU.
2. **Continental CrossContact LX Sport (30 sizes):** Overpriced by $10-$150+ on most sizes. Competitors sell at or below our wholesale cost.
3. **Continental CrossContact RX (89 sizes):** Overpriced on larger sizes, competitive on smaller sizes. Mixed bag.
4. **Continental ProContact RX (83 sizes):** Overpriced on most sizes by $30-$130.
5. **Yokohama Geolandar X-AT G016 (94 sizes):** UNDERPRICED -- massive margin opportunity. Raise prices by 30-60%.
6. **Pirelli Scorpion Zero All Season (39 sizes):** Overpriced by $50-$200+ per tire on most sizes.
7. **Pirelli P Zero AS Plus 3 (148 sizes):** Mixed -- small sizes competitive, large sizes overpriced.
8. **Yokohama Geolandar X-CV (32 sizes):** UNDERPRICED -- raise prices on most sizes.

## ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS

### Why are so many models overpriced?

1. **TDG wholesale pricing is not competitive on Continental, Pirelli, and large Falken/Bridgestone sizes.** Our wholesale costs on these brands are often AT or ABOVE what Canadian online retailers charge at retail. This is likely because TDG is a regional distributor with lower volume than the national chains.

2. **The blanket 30% markup ignores market dynamics.** A flat 30% works for Firestone, WeatherPeak, and Weathergrip because those are competitively-wholesaled. It FAILS for Continental and Pirelli where wholesale is already high.

3. **Large rim sizes (R20+) have the worst economics.** The wholesale cost gap widens on premium sizes. Competitors benefit from manufacturer volume rebates that TDG may not receive.

### Recommended Pricing Strategy

Instead of a flat 30% markup, implement a **tiered markup by brand and size**:

| Brand | R14-R16 | R17-R18 | R19-R20 | R21-R22 | R23+ |
|-------|---------|---------|---------|---------|------|
| Continental | 25% | 20% | 10% | 5% | 0-5% |
| Pirelli | 25% | 20% | 10% | 5% | 0-5% |
| Bridgestone | 30% | 28% | 25% | 20% | 15% |
| Firestone | 30% | 30% | 30% | 28% | 25% |
| Yokohama X-AT | 60% | 55% | 50% | 45% | 40% |
| Yokohama X-CV | 40% | 35% | 30% | 25% | 20% |
